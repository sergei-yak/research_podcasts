Machine Learning in Healthcare

Machine learning is transforming modern healthcare by enabling computers to learn from medical data without being explicitly programmed. This technology can analyze patient records, medical images, and clinical data to identify patterns and make predictions.

Key applications include diagnostic imaging, where AI can detect diseases in X-rays and MRIs, and personalized treatment recommendations based on patient history and genetics.

The future of healthcare will likely see increased integration of AI systems to improve patient outcomes and reduce costs.
